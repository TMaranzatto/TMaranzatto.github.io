I'm a postdoctoral researcher at University of Maryland. I find myself working on problems in learning theory, graph theory, and information theory. I'm currently working on the foundations of Large Language Models (LLMs), especially from a dynamical-systems angle.  I have spent a significant amount of my time studying gossiping networks (a type of communication protocol) from a statistical physics perspective, and I have a continued interest in this direction.


My current appointment is in [Sennur Ulukus's](https://user.eng.umd.edu/~ulukus/) lab in the Electrical and Computer Engineering department. I received my PhD in Mathematics from University of Illinois Chicago in 2024, advised by [Lev Reyzin](https://www.levreyzin.com/). 

**Thomas vs. Jake**: My legal name is Thomas and I use this as my primary identifier in my papers.  However in social settings I go by my middle name Jacob/ Jake.
